# Surface: websites/index.html (Websites für lokale Betriebe)

Mode: Persuade. A sub-page of the Bergmann AI site that sells the website offer.

- Audience: owners and office leads of small local businesses, mostly arriving from a personal cold email, often on the phone.
- Job and action: understand what the new website does for their customers, what is included, how it runs and what it costs; then "Website anfragen" (request dialog to the existing n8n webhook, mailto fallback).
- Proof: no testimonials, references or figures exist. The page demonstrates instead: a phone whose old site is repaired step by step. Facts only from PRODUCT.md and the confirmed homepage copy.
- Owner decisions (18.11.2026): structure Vorher/Nachher; look dark and light sections in alternation (Bergmann AI colours and faces); no examples section until real client sites exist; a short four-step process is wanted on this page.
- Constraints: prices only from the middle of the page; no invented proof; Sie; no kicker labels; one filled button per action group.
- Memorable moment: the Wandel section (later the one 3D scene).
- Build notes (18.11.2026): one split line for the page (12 columns, right column at line 7); the owner line lost its logo for the taste edge rule; the dawn is a background layer. DESIGN.md is updated after the 3D round.
- 3D (owner choice 20.11.2026): one 3D phone (three.js) travels across the whole page on a fixed canvas between the section grounds and the text. - Open: DESIGN.md merge for the light sections and the 3D phone (ask the owner); open owner questions listed in the report (domain ownership, what the client supplies, pages included, typical setup time).

## Direction contract

It refuses the category default of a split laptop hero, a row of same-size feature cards and a testimonial strip.

OWN-WORLD: Bergmann AI's evening edition extended into daylight. Night sections (#06070a) carry ivory type and champagne marks; ivory sections (#f3eee2) carry ink type and a darkened champagne for marks. No boxes, frames, glass or glow; one filled button per group (ivory on night, ink on ivory), every other action a text link with a drawn arrow; thin line icons; the phone is the only rounded object.

STORY: A business owner sees in seconds that this sells a new website for businesses like theirs, watches the phone turn an old site into one customers can read, call from and ask, learns what is included and how it runs, then reads two prices and sends a request.
