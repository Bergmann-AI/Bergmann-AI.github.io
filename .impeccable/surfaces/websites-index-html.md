## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the offer by letting the visitor scroll a finished premium website: one phone beside the reader that runs the owner's template live, scrolling as the page scrolls (on desktop the phone itself stands still), while the ground turns from cream to ivory. It refuses the category default of a split laptop hero, a row of same-size feature cards and a testimonial strip.

OWN-WORLD: Bergmann AI's evening edition, set in daylight (since 24.11.2026; before: chocolate brown, before that night). Cream sections (#ead9bd, the owner's colour one step darker) and ivory sections (#f3eee2) take turns; both carry ink type, a warm dark reading grey and a darkened champagne (bronze) for marks. Instrument Serif 400 headings with an italic continuation, Work Sans text. No boxes, frames, glass or glow; one filled button per group (ivory on night on the home page; on this page brown leather with ivory text since 25.11.2026), every other action a text link with a drawn arrow; thin line icons; the phone is the only rounded object.

STORY: A business owner sees in seconds that this sells a new website for businesses like theirs, scrolls a finished template on the phone while reading what a new site does (readable, one tap to call, fast, a chatbot at night), learns what is included and how it runs, then reads two prices and sends a request.
